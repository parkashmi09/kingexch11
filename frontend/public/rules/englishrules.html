11) RUN DIFFERENT:-
  • If both teams have the same score, the team that scored the most runs using the fewest number of balls will be declared the winner.
  • In this market, the team with the higher total runs based on selected player positions will be declared the winner.
  • For example:
    • Team 1: (IND1 + IND3 + AUS2 + AUS4)
    • Team 2: (IND2 + IND4 + AUS1 + AUS3)
  • This means Team 1’s total is the sum of runs scored by India’s 1st batter, India’s 3rd batter, Australia’s 2nd batter, and Australia’s 4th batter.
  • Team 2’s total is the sum of runs scored by India’s 2nd batter, India’s 4th batter, Australia’s 1st batter, and Australia’s 3rd batter.
  • The team with the higher total run from these selected players will be declared the winner.
  • NOTE :- In this situation, if both the runs and the number of balls faced by the two teams are equal, then the team with the highest number of boundaries will be declared the winner.
  • For example :- ( TEAM - 1 ) has 10 boundaries and ( TEAM - 2 ) has 12 boundaries then ( TEAM - 2 ) becomes the winner..
